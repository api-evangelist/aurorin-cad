# Aurorin CAD

> Aurorin CAD is a San Francisco company (YC W26, founded 2026 by Michael Baron) building a native
> desktop mechanical CAD application for macOS and Windows. It is built on a custom parametric,
> B-Rep driven CAD kernel written to exploit modern CPUs and GPUs and to be AI-native, pairing
> direct/parametric editing with an integrated AI agent. Aurorin publishes no public API, SDK, or
> developer documentation as of 2026-07-19.
